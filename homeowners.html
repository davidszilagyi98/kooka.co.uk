Our services

At Kooka, we specialise in high-quality insulation and rendering solutions designed specifically for homeowners. Our expert services include external wall insulation, Internal Wall Insulation, Cavity Wall Extraction, Silicone Rendering, and Loft Insulation, all tailored to enhance your home’s comfort and efficiency.

Currently, we're proud to be involved in the Watford Housing scheme, demonstrating our commitment to sustainability and energy efficiency. Our solutions are designed to not only boost your home's aesthetic appeal and longevity but also to improve its energy efficiency and occupant comfort.

Partner with Kooka to experience exceptional craftsmanship and personalized service, ensuring your home project meets the highest standards of quality and efficiency. Let us help you create a beautiful, energy-efficient home that truly stands out.
